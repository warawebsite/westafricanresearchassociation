WARA's Mission

Research

Foster the production and dissemination of current research on West Africa and the diaspora

Exchange

Promote scholarly exchange among West African scholars and institutions and their counterparts in the US and beyond

Global Awareness

Provide US audiences with accurate information on events and developments in West Africa and increase awareness of the critical place of West Africa in the global community

About Us

WARA is a non-profit membership organization dedicated to the promotion of research on West Africa and the diaspora, scholarly exchange, and the dissemination of accurate information about West Africa and its diasporic communities.
